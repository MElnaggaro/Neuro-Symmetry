Prodromal Sentinel

Pre-onset early-warning screen for facial nerve asymmetry — detects micro-lag in muscle activation before full palsy becomes visible.

Why this matters: Bell's Palsy onset is often preceded by subtle temporal asymmetry in eye closure and smile initiation, sometimes 48–72 hours before visible drooping. Combined with ipsilateral mastoid pain, hyperacusis, or taste change (chorda tympani involvement), temporal asymmetry > 200 ms is a clinically significant early signal.

How the assessment works

  1. Position your face in the camera frame so your whole face is visible
  2. Hold still for 1 second — the system establishes your baseline
  3. When prompted BLINK, close both eyes hard once
  4. When prompted SMILE, smile as broadly as you can
  5. Answer 3 quick symptom questions
  6. Receive your asymmetry analysis and alert status
