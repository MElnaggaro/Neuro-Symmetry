Mirror Neuron Therapy: Viewing a mirrored image of the healthy side of the face activates mirror neuron circuits in the motor cortex, promoting re-innervation of the affected facial nerve pathways. Clinical studies show improved House-Brackmann scores within 4–6 weeks of daily 15-minute sessions. The system mirrors the healthy half over the affected half in real-time, creating the perceptual illusion of bilateral symmetry.
