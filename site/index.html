Mission

Every second is brain tissue. We give clinicians and caregivers a head start.

Stroke and facial palsy leave their first signature on the face — a millimeter of droop, a fraction of a second of asymmetric motion. Neuro-Symmetry watches for those signals continuously, with the camera you already own, and surfaces a calibrated risk signal long before symptoms become unmistakable.

It is an assistive early-warning tool — designed to bring help sooner, not to replace the clinician who arrives next.

Pipeline

Four stages, end to end.

01

Capture

A standard webcam feed is normalized, face-aligned, and stabilized in real time — no special sensors, no controlled lighting.

02

Landmark Geometry

High-density facial landmarks are extracted; left/right correspondences are measured against the patient's own neutral baseline.

03

Asymmetry Score

Geometric and learned-feature streams converge through token-attention and squeeze-excitation blocks into a calibrated probability.

04

Risk Signal

A temporal smoother converts noisy per-frame estimates into a stable, decision-ready signal — paired with a region-level explanation.
