Neuro-Symmetry

A multimodal neurological signal engine that surfaces facial asymmetry — and the seconds you can't afford to miss — from a standard webcam.

The problem
0.0M
neurons lost · per minute · untreated stroke

Time-to-treatment is the single largest determinant of outcome. The first signs appear on the face — but the people who notice them are rarely trained to act in time.

The insight

Faces betray neurology before words do.

A millimeter of droop. A fraction of a second of asymmetric motion. With dense facial landmarks and learned features, these become measurable in real time — without specialized hardware.

What we built

A clinical-grade signal, from a webcam.

Real-Time Detection

Per-frame landmark geometry plus learned features run at 30 FPS on commodity hardware — no GPU required.

Latency · ~33 ms

Multimodal Fusion

Geometric asymmetry, texture cues, and temporal motion are fused with attention-weighted, hard-negative-aware training.

FocalLoss · ArcFace

Explainable Outputs
